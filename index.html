Key features for Canvas LMS

Dark mode & themes

Fully customizable dark mode. You can choose from many user-made themes or fine-tune every aspect yourself. You can even edit other people's themes for free.

A better todo list

Keep track of everything you need to do. Assignments, due dates and your own tasks in one clean interactive list.

GPA calculator

See your GPA at a glance, with presets for both high school and college grading scales. No more guessing what your grades add up to.

Grade history & analytics

Watch how your grades change over time and dig into the details. Use Imagine-If grades to see exactly what that next assignment would do to your grade, even if it was not added by your teacher.

Make Canvas yours

Custom backgrounds, dashboard notes with markdown, custom card styles, custom fonts, a better sidebar and global search across all your courses.

Frequently asked questions

Is Canvas Refined a replacement for BetterCanvas, BetterCampus or Super Canvas?

It works as a drop-in replacement with all the great features and more. It is also compatible with existing BetterCanvas themes.

Does Canvas Refined send my data anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser: your courses, grades, tasks and theme settings never leave your device, and the extension has no analytics, tracking pixels or servers.

Which browsers can I install Canvas Refined on?

It is published on the Chrome Web Store (which also covers Chromium browsers such as Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi) and on Firefox Add-ons.

Does it work with my school's Canvas site?

Canvas Refined works on Canvas LMS, so it runs on school-hosted instances such as yourschool.instructure.com as well as self-hosted Canvas domains. If a school-managed device blocks browser extensions you may need to install it on a personal device or browser profile.

Does this work for Brightspace, Blackboard or Google Classroom?

No, Canvas Refined only works on Canvas LMS. If anyone wants to port it to other platforms, you are welcome to!
